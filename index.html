Our Story

Before we celebrate everything we've become, let's go back to the moment it all started.

assets/photos/how-we-met.jpg

How We Met

Some stories begin with grand gestures, while ours began with something beautifully simple—a mutual friend who unknowingly changed our lives forever. I never imagined that one conversation, one interaction and one smile would slowly become my favorite place to be. Looking back now, it feels like fate quietly worked behind the scenes, bringing two hearts together at exactly the right moment. That single introduction wasn't just how we met—it was the beginning of every beautiful memory we've created, every laugh we've shared, every challenge we've faced together, and every dream we now hold hand in hand. If I could relive one moment over and over again, I'd choose that day, because it led me to you—the best thing that has ever happened to me.

assets/photos/first-conversation.jpg

Our First Conversation

It started with just a few simple words, but little did we know that those words would become the beginning of our forever. What seemed like an ordinary conversation slowly turned into hours of endless chats, shared smiles through the screen, and a comfort that neither of us had ever expected. Somewhere between the "hellos" and the "goodnights," you became my favorite notification, my safest place, and the person I couldn't wait to talk to every single day. Looking back now, I realize it was never just our first conversation—it was the first page of the most beautiful love story I'll ever be a part of.

assets/photos/becoming-friends.jpg

How We Became Friends

We didn't become friends overnight. It happened quietly, one conversation at a time, one laugh after another, and one memory after the next. What started as simple talks slowly turned into sharing everything—our happiest moments, our worries, our dreams, and even our silence. Without realizing it, you became the person I looked forward to talking to every day. Somewhere along the way, friendship stopped feeling like just a word and became a beautiful bond that made life brighter. Looking back now, I truly believe that our friendship wasn't just a coincidence—it was love finding its own way to begin.

assets/photos/falling-in-love.jpg

How We Fell In Love

I never thought that you will fall in love with me—But it simply happened, quietly and beautifully. Somewhere between the endless conversations, the late-night chats, the inside jokes, and the little moments that made us smile, our hearts found each other. Every day, I discovered another reason to admire you, to care for you, and to choose you. What began as friendship slowly became something neither of us could ignore. Love didn't arrive all at once; it grew with every memory we created, every promise we made, and every moment we stood by each other. And before we even realized it, you had become my favorite person, my greatest comfort, and the most beautiful chapter of my life.

assets/photos/first-date.jpg

Our First Date

Our first date will forever be one of the most precious memories of my life. No matter how many years pass, no matter if we get married, build a beautiful life together, or create thousands of unforgettable moments, I know one thing for sure—our first date will never fade from my heart. It will always hold a special place that nothing else can ever replace. I still remember every little emotion I felt that day—the nervousness, the excitement, the endless smiles, and the butterflies in my stomach while waiting to see you. All I wanted was to meet you, look into your eyes, and finally hold your hand. It felt like time had slowed down just for us.
And then i saw you...
You looked absolutely adorable in your black top and blue jeans. I don't know if you realized it, but the moment I saw you, I couldn't take my eyes off you. You were the most beautiful person in the world, and all my nervousness suddenly felt worth it because I was finally with the girl I had been waiting to see. Every conversation, every smile, every laugh, every little silence, and every moment we spent together became a memory I never want to lose. That day wasn't just our first date—it was the beginning of countless beautiful memories that we would create together. My only wish is that no matter how many dates we go on in the future, every single one of them carries the same magic, excitement, and happiness that our very first date gave us. Because if I could relive one day over and over again, I would always choose the day I fell in love with you all over again.

assets/photos/our-journey.jpg

Our Journey So Far

Today marks one beautiful year of us. One year of love, laughter, memories, dreams, and a million emotions that words can never fully describe. If I look back at everything we've been through, I realize that our journey hasn't been perfect—but maybe that's what makes it so real and so beautiful. We've laughed until our stomachs hurt, smiled over the smallest things, cried together, celebrated little victories, and stood beside each other even during the difficult days. I know that in the past few weeks, I've made mistakes that hurt you deeply. I wish I could erase those moments because seeing pain in your eyes is the last thing I ever want. From the bottom of my heart, I'm truly sorry. Thank you for loving me even when I wasn't the best version of myself. Today, I don't just want to celebrate one year of our relationship—I want to make you a promise. I promise to keep learning from my mistakes, to understand you better, to protect your heart, and to become someone who gives you more reasons to smile than to cry. I know I won't become perfect overnight, but I promise that every single day I'll try to be a better boyfriend than I was yesterday. With every passing month, our love has grown stronger. Every conversation, every late-night call, every little fight, every apology, every "I miss you," every hug, every kiss, every date, and every moment we've shared has become a part of the story that I will cherish for the rest of my life. These memories are treasures that time can never take away.

And the most beautiful part is... this is only the beginning.

There are still countless chapters waiting for us. There are sunsets we haven't watched together, cities we haven't explored hand in hand, cafés waiting for our endless conversations, long drives with our favorite songs playing, movie nights where you'll steal my hoodie, festivals we'll celebrate together, birthdays I'll make unforgettable, surprise dates, silly shopping trips, rainy evening walks, countless photographs, vacations to our dream destinations, and one day, the beautiful life we'll build together—filled with love, laughter, and a lifetime of memories. Years from now, when we look back at this first year, I hope we'll smile and realize that every challenge only made our love stronger. Because no matter how difficult some days become, one thing will always remain the same—

If I had the chance to choose again, in every lifetime, I would still choose you.

Here's to our first year... and to forever, my love. ❤️
